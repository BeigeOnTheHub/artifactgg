Privacy & legal

artifactgg.com (also vailscan.gg) is a free fan-made planner for Vail VR strats. Last updated November 2026.

Who runs it

One person from the Vail community, as a hobby. There's no company behind it and nobody makes money from it. Questions, takedown requests and anything else go through the contact link at the bottom. If you're in the EU or UK you can also complain to your data protection authority, but getting in touch first is usually quicker.

Terms of use

  • You need to be at least 13 to use it.
  • What you put in a strat is your responsibility: player names, notes, text, and anything you send round in a link.
  • Don't use it for anything illegal. Don't put other people's personal details or copyrighted work in a strat (the game's own maps are fine), and don't use it to harass anyone.
  • Don't try to break it or overload it, and don't use it to spread malware or spam.
  • The site can change or close at any time, and so can these terms. If you keep using it after a change, you're agreeing to the new version. If you don't agree with them, please don't use the site.

Not affiliated with Vail

This is a community project. It's not made, run, or endorsed by AEXLAB or the Vail VR team. Vail, VAIL VR and the map artwork belong to their owners and are only here so people can plan. If you own something used here and want it gone, get in touch and it'll be taken down.

Copyright and takedowns

To report something, use the contact link or the report button below. It helps if you include:

  • what you own;
  • where it shows up here: paste the whole share link (the strat is inside it), or say which asset;
  • how to reach you;
  • that you believe in good faith the use isn't allowed by you or by law;
  • that the report is accurate and you own the rights or act for whoever does.

If something of yours was taken down and you think that was a mistake, send a counter-notice through the same channel saying why, with your contact details, and it'll be looked at again. Reports are read by one person rather than a registered copyright agent, but valid ones get acted on.

Your data

Saved strats, settings and preferences stay in your browser's local storage. Nothing is sent anywhere and there are no accounts, so there's no copy on a server either. Clearing your browser data or switching device loses them, so use Export .strat.json for anything you want to keep.

Share links

A share link has the whole strat packed inside it. Nothing is uploaded, so there's nothing on our side to delete. Anyone with the link sees everything in the strat, names and notes included, and can pass it on. To stop sharing, stop handing the link out. People who already have it keep their copy.

No tracking

No analytics, ads, cookies, fingerprinting or third-party scripts. Fonts and map art ship with the app, so loading the page doesn't contact Google or anyone else, and once it's loaded the app makes no network requests of its own.
