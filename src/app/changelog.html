What's new in v2.2.1

  • Kiiro has callouts now, and it's no longer marked as a work in progress.
  • Switching maps fits the whole map in the window instead of opening too big.
  • No more scrollbars round the edge of the page.
  • Paste a share link into a tab that's already open and it loads. If you've got unsaved work it asks first.
  • Undo after opening or importing a strat can't bring the old one back any more.
  • The eraser catches thin lines on a quick swipe.
  • The privacy and legal page is shorter and easier to read.
  • Plus a load of smaller bug fixes, mostly around undo, saving and dragging things about.

v2.2.0

Mostly mobile. The touch layout could show a strat but not build one. Everything below is new since v2.1.0.

Things you couldn't do on a phone before

  • Change map, and turn callouts on and off.
  • Toggle layers, reset the view and clear a frame, from a View panel.
  • Write and edit strat notes.
  • Select a drawn line. Only a mouse could pick those up.
  • Select a player to change their team, name or dead marker, or a cone to change its spread.

Sideways

  • Landscape has its own layout rather than the portrait one stretched out.
  • The tool rail goes to two columns, so every tool stays on screen.
  • Panels sit in a column down the right instead of a band across the bottom.
  • An open panel no longer covers the frame strip, the top bar or the tool rail.
  • The colour wheel fits without scrolling.

Gestures

  • Two fingers moving together pan the map. Before, only a pinch did anything.
  • Double-tap zooms out to the whole map, and again to go back in.
  • Press and hold anything you've placed for a menu with Delete, Duplicate and Add to selection.
  • Two-finger tap undoes.
  • The multi-select button has gone. Add to selection in the hold menu turns the mode on; tap the map to leave it.

Working with a finger

  • Tap to select and drag to move.
  • Tap targets are finger-sized.
  • Drag handles on cones and shapes no longer shrink to nothing as you zoom out.
  • Only one panel opens at a time, and tapping the map closes it.
  • Portrait opens zoomed in far enough to read.

Text boxes

  • Placing one opens a normal-sized field in the panel, rather than a box on the map about five pixels tall.
  • The words appear on the map as you type, and a burst of typing is one undo step.
  • The panel lifts clear of the on-screen keyboard.
  • A new text box no longer deletes itself when you reach for the field.

Not just mobile

  • Selected items glow, on a phone and on desktop, so you can tell what you're about to move.
  • Select a player or a cone and its settings show in the sidebar.
  • Placing a text box selects it, so its settings are there straight away.

And other UI changes

  • The menu leads with Map, View and Notes instead of burying them under File and Export.
  • The onion-skin button has a proper icon.
  • The top bar and frame strip are slimmer sideways, so there's more map.
  • Nothing hides under a notch or a rounded corner.
  • The player legend keeps clear of the tool rail, and hides when nobody is named.
  • Frame cards no longer overlap on a narrow screen.
  • A panel's close button no longer sits on top of a control.
